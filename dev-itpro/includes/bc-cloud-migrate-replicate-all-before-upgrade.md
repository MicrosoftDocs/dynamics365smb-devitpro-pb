> [!IMPORTANT]
> Before running data upgrade, ensure that data from all companies that you plan to migrate has been replicated to the online database. Once a company's upgrade has started, you can't replicate that company again from an earlier version. Mixing nonupgraded records with upgraded records risks corrupting data in the online database.
