You can migrate all the data that you want to take with you to the cloud, but be aware of the storage capacity of your online tenant. If you need more storage than the default 80 GB, you can buy another environment or extra storage capacity. Storage capacity includes 80 GB, allowances per Essentials, Premium, Device, or Partner Sandbox license, and extra capacity from purchased environments and storage capacity licenses.
