# Early access preview environments in Business Central

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

This article introduces early access preview environments in [!INCLUDE [prod_short](../developer/includes/prod_short.md)]. You can spin up an early access preview sandbox of [!INCLUDE [prod_short](../developer/includes/prod_short.md)] online to test new features and provide feedback before they're generally available. The environments in early access preview are provided as-is and are subject to change.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Early access preview environments are available only for tenants with a [Dynamics 365 Business Central Partner Sandbox license](../administration/environment-types.md#partnersandbox) and for United States (US), United Kingdom (GB), and Germany (DE) versions.
> - Preview features aren't meant for production use and might have restricted functionality. These features are subject to [supplemental terms of use](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2105274).
> - Early access preview environments are provided for evaluation purposes only so partners can try features before public release and provide feedback.
